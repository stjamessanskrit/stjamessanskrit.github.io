Sanskrit Paradigms Trainer

Learn every form, one table at a time

How to use the trainer

Pick a table from the list: a noun; a pronoun (tad, asmad, or yuṣmad); or a verb in the present, past, or future, either bhavati with parasmaipada endings or vardhate with ātmanepada endings. Each table has four steps.

  1. ① Study. Tap any form to see what it means. ✂️ Endings shows the ending of each form, and 👯 Spot the twins colours the forms that look the same. When you feel ready, turn on 🙈 Cover and recall: say each form aloud, then tap to check. Flip every card to earn your Study tick.
  2. ② Build the table. The gold box shows which form to find. Tap the right tile at the bottom to fill it in. You can tap any empty box to choose a different one. Beat the clock and make as few mistakes as you can.
  3. ③ Make the form. You are told the case and number (or person and number). Choose the right Sanskrit form.
  4. ④ Name the form. You see a Sanskrit form. Choose its case and number. Some forms can be more than one case; the app shows you all of them afterwards.

Earning rewards

  • Every right answer earns XP. Get answers right in a row to build a combo: ×2 after 3, ×3 after 6, and ×4 after 10.
  • Each of steps ② to ④ earns up to 3 stars, so every table has 9 stars to collect.
  • Reach 100 XP in a day to hit your daily goal and keep your 🔥 streak going.
  • ⚡ 60-second Blitz mixes every table. A wrong answer costs 3 seconds. How high can you score?
  • Use 50:50 if you are stuck: it removes two wrong answers, but you earn half the XP.
Keyboard: press 1, 2, 3, or 4 to choose an answer and Enter to move on. The app remembers which forms you find hard and asks you those more often.
